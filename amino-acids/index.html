Proteinogenic Amino Acids

The 21 proteinogenic α-amino acids in their zwitterionic forms, with side-chain protonation states at physiological pH (7.4).

Amino acid composition and roles in binding

Frequencies below are approximate, from UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot averages. They vary by proteome, and membrane proteins and intrinsically disordered regions (IDRs) deviate a lot (see notes after the table).

Key patterns

PPI hotspots. Bogan & Thorn (1998) found that hotspots are enriched in Trp, Arg, and Tyr, with Ile, Asp, and Pro also contributing. Leu, Ser, Thr, and Val are comparatively underrepresented. Hotspots tend to be buried and ringed by hydrophobic residues that exclude water ("O-ring" model). This is why PPI inhibitors often need to mimic Trp/Leu/Phe side chains (e.g. p53–MDM2: Phe19, Trp23, Leu26 sit in the MDM2 cleft).

Ligand pockets. Binding sites are consistently enriched in the larger aromatic and polarizable residues (Trp, Tyr, Phe, His, Met, Cys) plus Arg/Asp for specific polar anchors. They are depleted in Lys, Glu, Gln, and Pro, which are more surface-typical.

Druggability and chemistry.

  • Nucleophiles for covalent chemistry: Cys (most common), Lys, Tyr, His, Ser, and Thr.
  • Charged anchors for selectivity: Asp/Glu/Arg/Lys give directional salt bridges that are hard to get from hydrophobic contacts alone.
  • Gatekeeper residues (Thr/Met/Phe/Leu) shape kinase selectivity.

Composition caveats.

  • Transmembrane proteins are enriched in Leu, Ile, Val, Phe, Ala, and Gly.
  • IDRs are enriched in Ser, Pro, Glu, Gln, Lys, and Gly, and depleted in Trp, Phe, Tyr, Ile, Leu, and Cys.

Practical notes for modeling and docking

  • His tautomer and protonation state (HID/HIE/HIP) can change docking poses and scores substantially.
  • Asn/Gln/His flips should be checked, since crystal structures often assign amide orientation arbitrarily.
  • Cys pKa varies widely (thiolate vs thiol), which matters for covalent docking.
  • Lys/Arg/Asp/Glu protonation can shift in buried pockets, so PROPKA-type estimates are worth running.
